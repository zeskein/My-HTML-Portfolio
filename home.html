My Story

I’m an educator passionate about helping students grow both in knowledge and character. My journey as an instructor has shaped me into a purpose-driven professional who values learning, creativity, and connection. With the support of my institution, I’ve earned my Master Expert in UI/UX Design certification through Simplilearn, completed an industry apprenticeship that enhanced my teaching practice, and developed modules and learning materials currently used by students. Beyond the classroom, I’ve proudly served as a Council Adviser for over five years, mentoring student leaders and fostering a culture of teamwork and service. Each experience continues to inspire me to guide learners not only to succeed academically but also to discover their potential and build meaningful careers.
